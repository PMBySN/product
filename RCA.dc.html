First, what even is an RCA?

A Root Cause Analysis is you refusing to stop at the symptom. Sign-ups dropped 20%? That's the symptom. The root cause is the real, fixable thing hiding three questions deeper. RCA is simply the discipline of asking "why?" until you hit something you can actually change. Master this one skill and you instantly sound like a PM with years on you.

The framework I actually use

The 5-step CALM method

C
Clarify the problem.
Pin the exact metric, the exact drop, the exact timeframe. "Retention fell" is a headline; "D7 retention fell from 42% to 33% last Tuesday" is something you can chase.
A
Ask "is it real?"
Before you sprint, rule out the boring culprits — a tracking bug, a broken dashboard, a holiday. Half of all "crises" die right here. Celebrate when they do.
L
Layer it down.
Slice by platform, geography, new vs. returning, and release date. The root cause almost always lives inside one slice, not across all of them.
M
Map to a cause.
Ask "why?" until the answer is a decision, a change, or an event — internal (a shipped feature) or external (a competitor, a season). That's your root.
✦
Move on it.
A cause with no fix is trivia. Close with the action and the metric you'll watch to know it worked. Always end pointing forward.
