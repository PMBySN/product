Design is empathy, made decisive

Every "how would you design X?" is answering a silent question: whose life gets better, and how? So we don't start with screens — we start with the human, pick the one who needs it most, find the pain that hurts most, and choose the fix with the biggest payoff. Commit to those calls out loud and you sound like a product leader.

The design-sense playbook

The 6-step method

1
Clarify.
Ask the questions that pin down scope, users and constraints.
2
Meet the users, pick one.
List real personas, then commit to the one with the most to gain.
3
Find the pains.
What actually hurts them, ranked by frustration × frequency.
4
Generate solutions.
Ideas against the top pain — quantity first, then cut.
5
Prioritise.
RICE or Impact/Effort. Deciding is the job — show your reasoning.
6
Define metrics.
How you'll know the design actually worked.
