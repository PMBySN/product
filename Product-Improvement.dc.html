The method I run every time

The 8-beat improvement method

1
Describe the product.
Say what it does and who it's for, in a sentence. Align before you analyse.
2
Clarify.
Ask the questions that shrink the scope — goals, constraints, current pains.
3
Set the goal.
One crisp line: what does 'better' actually mean here?
4
Pick the users.
Name real segments — never 'everyone.' Specific beats broad.
5
List the pain points.
What actually hurts them today, ranked by how often and how badly.
6
Generate solutions.
Brainstorm freely against those pains — quantity first, then cut.
7
Prioritise with RICE.
Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Deciding is the job — show your math.
8
Define metrics.
How you'll know it worked. A fix you don't measure is a wish.
