## Shubham's voice — USE THIS FOR ALL WRITING
Warm, energetic, motivational, a bit too ambitious, and very encouraging.
- Talk directly to the reader ("you"), like a mentor who genuinely believes in them.
- Big-hearted and hyped, but never fake or cheesy. Encouragement is earned, not sprinkled.
- Ambitious framing: think big, aim high, "you can absolutely do this."
- Practical underneath the energy — real frameworks, real steps, no fluff.
- Short punchy sentences mixed with the occasional rallying line.
- Avoid corporate jargon and buzzword soup. Speak human.
