FREE RESOURCE · GUESSTIMATES

Estimate anything, calmly.

"How many electric scooters are in Bangalore?" The interviewer isn't testing whether you know — nobody knows. They're watching how your brain breaks a scary question into friendly pieces. Learn the skeleton once and you'll walk into any guesstimate grinning instead of sweating. Let's get you there.

THE METHOD + 25 SOLVED·WITH FUNNELS & FIGURES

The mindset shift

Stop trying to be right. Start trying to be reasonable and clear. A guesstimate is a story you tell out loud: here's my starting number, here's how I split it, here are my assumptions. Say your assumptions with confidence and the exact answer barely matters. That's the single biggest unlock — and it's entirely in your control.

The skeleton I use every time

The A-B-S-C method

A
Anchor.
Start from one big number you trust — a population, a market size, a household count. Everything hangs off this hook.
B
Break it down.
Split the anchor into clean segments — by access, income, behaviour, market share. A funnel of simple fractions beats one wild guess.
S
State assumptions.
Say each percentage out loud and why. Owning your assumptions is what earns the points — not being magically correct.
C
Check reality.
Step back and sniff-test the total. Off by 100×? Occupancy over 100%? Find the assumption that broke. This move separates pros from panickers.
