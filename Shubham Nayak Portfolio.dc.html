01 — About

Hi, I'm Shubham — I like turning messy problems into shipped products.

I'm a Product Manager at Scholarin, where I work across discovery, roadmapping and launch — living in the space between what users struggle with and what the team can build next. I care about crisp problem framing, honest metrics, and shipping things that actually move the needle.

Alongside the day job, I document everything I learn. This site doubles as a free resource hub for students and early-career folks trying to break into product — the RCAs, guesstimates and frameworks I wish I'd had on day one.
