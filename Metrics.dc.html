Vanity vs. value, in one line

A vanity metric makes you feel good. A value metric tells you whether users got what they came for. Total sign-ups? Feels great, means little. Users who hit their aha-moment this week? Now we're talking. The whole art is learning to tell them apart — and it's a skill, which means you can absolutely learn it.

The framework I use in every metrics question

The 4-layer metric stack

1
North Star.
The single number that captures real value delivered — the moment users actually win. Not revenue, not raw users. Everything else serves this.
2
Inputs.
The few levers your team can actually pull that move the North Star. This is what your roadmap is really about.
3
Guardrails.
The lines you refuse to cross while chasing growth — churn, latency, trust, support load. Winning ugly isn't winning.
4
Diagnostics.
The detailed cuts you open only when something moves. Great for answers, dangerous as a daily dashboard.
